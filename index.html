Intentions, Learning Experiences, Assessment, & Ways Forward Framework

School: Mabini National High School Grade Level: Grade 8
Teacher: Maria L. Santos, Master Teacher I Learning Area: Science
Quarter/Week: Quarter 1 - Week 3 Duration: 60 Minutes
Lesson Topic
Photosynthesis: Plant Food Making Process
I. INTENTIONS (Layunin) What learners should know, feel & do
Content Standard:

Demonstrate understanding of plant cell structures involved in energy transformation.

Performance Standard:

Design a visual diagram illustrating the inputs and outputs of light reaction.

Learning Competency:

S8LT-IVf-16: Differentiate the basic features of photosynthesis and cell respiration, and describe how plants transform light energy into chemical energy.

Unpacked Learning Objectives (KSA):
  • Knowledge (K): Explain the role of chlorophyll, sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide in photosynthesis.
  • Skills (S): Construct a balanced chemical reaction equation diagram for photosynthesis in a group setup.
  • Attitude/Values (A): Demonstrate stewardship for green plants and environmental conservation.
II. LEARNING EXPERIENCES (Pagkakasunod-sunod ng Aralin) Gradual Release & Differentiated Flow
1. Pre-Lesson Hook & Motivation (5 mins):

Present two potted plants—one healthy green plant kept in sunlight, and one wilted yellow plant kept in a dark closet for 5 days. Ask: "What makes the first plant thrive and create its own nourishment?"

2. Gradual Release Instructional Flow:
Direct Instruction ("I DO"):

Teacher presents an interactive diagram of the chloroplast, showing how sunlight splits water (H2O) and converts CO2 into glucose (C6H2O6) and Oxygen (O2).

Guided Practice ("WE DO"):

Students work in pairs to arrange word-card formula pieces (Sunlight + Water + CO2 → Glucose + Oxygen) into the correct sequence on their tables.

Independent Practice ("YOU DO"):

Learners individually complete a flow chart tracing how light energy transforms into chemical energy in plant leaves.

3. Differentiated Group Learning Activities:
Group A (Support Tier)

Fill in missing key terms on a guided diagram of photosynthesis with vocabulary helper cards.

Group B (Core Tier)

Construct a complete poster diagram showing light-dependent and light-independent reactions.

Group C (Extension Tier)

Predict the environmental impact if deforestation drastically cuts local plant photosynthesis rates.

Learning Resources & Materials: Potted plant specimens, Chloroplast visual diagram, Vocabulary flashcards, Activity worksheets, Manila paper & markers.
III. ASSESSMENT (Pagtataya) Formative Checks & HOTS
Formative Assessment Check:

5-item short check quiz & exit ticket: "Write the overall chemical equation for photosynthesis and name the primary pigment that absorbs light."

Higher-Order Thinking Skills (HOTS) Questions:
  1. Why would placing a aquatic plant under green-filtered light slow down oxygen bubble production?
  2. How does deforestation directly affect global oxygen supply through the lens of cellular photosynthesis?
Evaluation Criteria & Rubric:

Diagram Accuracy (40%), Scientific Explanation (30%), Group Collaboration (20%), Neatness & Timeliness (10%).

IV. WAYS FORWARD (Pagninilay at Pagpapalawig) Synthesis & Intervention
Lesson Synthesis / Generalization:

Learners summarize in 3 key sentences: Plants convert radiant solar energy into chemical energy stored in sugar, releasing vital oxygen as a byproduct.

Teacher & Student Reflection:

90% of learners achieved mastery in diagram identification; 10% require additional guided practice on chemical equations.

Remediation Task (Interbensyon):

Provide guided matching sheet connecting inputs (Water, Light, CO2) with outputs (Glucose, O2) for students below 75% score.

Enrichment / Extension (Pagpapalawig):

Research home indoor plants that are highly effective at absorbing indoor air pollutants overnight.
